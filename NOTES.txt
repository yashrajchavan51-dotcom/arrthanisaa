ARTHANISA DESIGN STUDIO — site files (revision 20)
==================================================

WHAT CHANGED IN THIS REVISION
-----------------------------
A visual redesign. Every word of copy, the page titles, link-preview
tags, structured data, the enquiry form (and its Web3Forms key), the
project filter and the menu behaviour are unchanged. What changed is
how it looks:

  Hero       The name set large in spaced capitals, with an
             architect's dimension line under it measuring the two
             Sanskrit parts: ARTHA (meaningful) + NISA (home). The
             lines draw in once on load; that is the only animation
             on the site. The townhouse photo sits below.
  About      Copy left, cathedral sketch right, paper blended into
             the page.
  Vision     Pantheon photo runs off the left edge of the page.
  Team       One row per founder: portrait frame, name and role,
             then the bio.
  Empanel.   A register: one row per authority, logo + caption.
  Expertise  The four services sit on one dimension chain, since
             they are stages in order (1 to 4).
  Quote      Same fixed-background behaviour; photo pulled to
             greyscale so the blue glass doesn't fight the gold.
  Projects   Filters as tabs. Empty photo slots carry architectural
             hatching instead of flat grey boxes.
  Footer     Laid out as a drawing title block (Get in Touch,
             address and contact, "Arthanisa since 2017").

Small presentation changes, no copy lost:
  - Header wordmark: ARTHANISA in one colour (the old version put
    "SA" in gold, which split the name in the wrong place).
  - Footer: email and phone on their own lines instead of joined
    with a middle dot.
  - The home page h1 is now the visible hero rather than hidden.

NOTE: the header still uses the old triangle-and-frame mark. When
the approved interlocking-frames logo is final, replace the inline
<svg class="logo-mark"> in all three HTML files (it appears twice
per page: header and footer) and favicon.svg.

the logo work. The creams are gone.

FONTS
-----
Two families, all self-hosted, no Google Fonts request.

  Pagella Regular + Italic   headings, quote, Sanskrit words, hero
                             labels. TeX Gyre Pagella (a Palatino
                             design), GUST Font License, free to use
                             and host. Subset to Latin, ~24 KB each.
  Absans (text cut)          running copy, buttons, nav. Subset to
                             Latin, ~40 KB.
  Absans (logo cut)          the ARTHANISA wordmark, capitals only.
